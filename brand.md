# Radio Ainur Brand

## Identity

Radio Ainur is a curated music archive focused on discovery, atmosphere, context, and personal curation.

## Core Ideas

- Music discovery
- Personal curation
- Atmosphere
- Emotional context
- Exploration
- Rediscovery

## Tone of Voice

Radio Ainur uses a concise, atmospheric, emotional, and understated tone.

The writing should complement the music rather than compete with it.

## Brand Principle

> Music should be discovered, experienced, remembered, and easy to return to.
