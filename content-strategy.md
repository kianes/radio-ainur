# Radio Ainur Content Strategy

## Content Structure

A typical Radio Ainur post may contain:

1. Artist
2. Track title
3. Short atmospheric description
4. Track number
5. Relevant hashtags

## Curation

Music is selected based on its artistic character, atmosphere, emotional quality, and relevance to the overall identity of the archive.

## Metadata

Hashtags can describe:

- Genre
- Style
- Mood
- Atmosphere
- Context

## Editorial Principle

The text should create an entry point into the music.

It should not overwhelm the listening experience.

## Discovery

Radio Ainur supports several discovery paths:

- Artist
- Track
- Genre
- Mood
- Atmosphere
- Cinematic context
- Direct Index navigation
