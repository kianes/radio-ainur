# Radio Ainur
 
### A Curated Music Archive & Discovery Project
 
**549 tracks · Curated selection · Editorial context · Digital archive**
 
Radio Ainur is an independent music curation project built around the discovery, organization, documentation, and long-term preservation of a curated music collection.
 
The project brings together music discovery, editorial context, metadata, and cross-platform navigation into a structured digital archive.
  
## 🎧 The Archive
 
The Radio Ainur archive currently contains **549 curated tracks**, organized by artist and connected directly to their original Telegram posts.
 
### Music Index
 
→ **Browse the complete 549-track Music Index**
 
The index provides:
 
 
- Artist-based organization
 
- Track numbering where available
 
- Direct links to original Telegram posts
 
- A structured, continuously maintainable catalogue
 

  
## 🧭 How the Archive Works
 
Radio Ainur treats music curation as more than publishing individual tracks.
 `Music Discovery        ↓ Selection        ↓ Curation        ↓ Editorial Context        ↓ Metadata        ↓ Track Numbering        ↓ Telegram Archive        ↓ GitHub Index        ↓ Spotify Listening ` 
The result is a connected system for discovering, documenting, and revisiting music.

## 🎼 Curation Philosophy
 
Radio Ainur is built around the idea that music should be:
 
**discovered, experienced, remembered, and revisited.**
 
The archive focuses on creating a collection that can be explored over time rather than functioning only as a chronological stream of publications.
