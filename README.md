# Radio Ainur

### A Curated Music Archive & Discovery Project

[![Telegram](https://img.shields.io/badge/Telegram-Radio%20Ainur-26A5E4?logo=telegram&logoColor=white)](https://t.me/radioainur)
[![Spotify](https://img.shields.io/badge/Spotify-Ainulindal%C3%AB-1DB954?logo=spotify&logoColor=white)](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/2WApbnha1PDsTealeyxBiw)

> **Music should be discovered, experienced, remembered, and easy to return to.**

Radio Ainur is an independent music curation project built around the idea of turning music discovery into a more personal, contextual, and navigable experience.

What began as a Telegram music channel has evolved into a curated archive of **549 tracks**, supported by track numbering, editorial descriptions, hashtags, and a dedicated clickable music index.

---

## 🎵 About Radio Ainur

Radio Ainur is not intended to be just another chronological music feed.

The project focuses on **curation, discovery, atmosphere, and rediscovery**.

Each track becomes part of a growing collection that can be explored through different entry points:

- Artist
- Track
- Genre
- Style
- Mood
- Atmosphere
- Cinematic or soundtrack context

The collection intentionally moves across different musical territories rather than being restricted to a single genre.

---

## 🎧 The Music

The archive includes a broad range of musical styles, including:

- Alternative
- Ambient
- Cinematic
- Electronic
- Instrumental
- Indie
- Progressive Rock
- Rock
- Neoclassical
- Soundtrack
- Jazz
- Heavy Metal
- Pop
- Folk
- and other related styles

The purpose is not to create strict genre boundaries, but to create connections between different kinds of listening experiences.

---

## ✍️ Editorial Context

Many Radio Ainur posts include a short editorial sentence designed to capture the atmosphere or emotional character of the track.

Rather than providing a conventional music review, the text acts as a small entry point into the listening experience.

For example:

**Sebastian Plano — Fortanach**

> Quietly moving through places without a name.

`#Ambient` `#Neoclassical` `#
